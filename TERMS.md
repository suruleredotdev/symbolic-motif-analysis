# Terms for shared image data

**Status: draft — permission from the Frobenius Institut has not yet been
obtained.** Treat this as the working agreement between collaborators, not as a
licence. Nothing here grants rights that the project does not itself hold.

## What is being shared

Subset bundles produced by `make_subset.sh` contain two kinds of material, with
different standing:

| | Origin | Standing |
|---|---|---|
| Source photographs (`images/`) | Frobenius Institut Bildarchiv | Rights held by the Institut. No licence has been granted to this project. |
| Derived crops (`analysis/`) | Cut and processed from the above | Derived works of the same photographs. Being derived does not clear the underlying rights. |

The source photographs were retrieved from the Bildarchiv's public web
interface, which is session-based and required no API key or account. No terms
of use were presented or accepted at retrieval time, which means the project is
operating without an explicit grant — not that the material is unrestricted.

## What collaborators agree to

By accepting access to a bundle:

1. Use the material for non-commercial research on this project only.
2. Do not redistribute it — not to colleagues, not to institutional
   repositories, not to model-training corpora, and not in public notebooks,
   preprints, or repositories.
3. Do not re-upload it to any third-party service, including hosted notebook
   platforms other than the shared Drive folder set up for this project.
4. Delete local copies when your involvement ends, if asked.
5. Reproductions in a paper or talk are a separate question — ask first, so
   permission can be sought for the specific images and context.

Access is granted to named individuals and is revocable.

## Attribution

Cite source photographs as: *Frobenius Institut, Bildarchiv, <catalogue
number>*. Catalogue numbers for a given bundle are listed in its `MANIFEST.md`.

## Outstanding

Written permission for non-commercial research use should be requested from the
Frobenius Institut, covering the specific catalogue numbers in circulation.
Until that is in hand, the restrictions above are the project's own
precaution and this file should not be read as a grant of rights. If the
Institut's terms turn out to be narrower than the above, theirs govern.
